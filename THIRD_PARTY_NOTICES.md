## Wikipedia contributors

[references/ai-writing-signals.md](references/ai-writing-signals.md) is an
adapted editorial taxonomy based on Wikipedia contributors' [Wikipedia:
Signs of AI writing](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing&oldid=1361516048),
revision 1361516048. The source attribution is also retained in that file.
The adaptation reorganizes and summarizes editorial signals and adds review
moves. This adapted reference is shared under [Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

## Other credited influences

The original skill credits George Orwell's 1946 essay, "Politics and the
English Language," for plain-language editing principles, and links to
[Vox's thread](https://x.com/Voxyz_ai/status/2078857039116156978) for practical
applications.

It also records a review of Jimmy Slagle's
[anti-ai-slop-scriptwriting](https://github.com/jimmyslagle/anti-ai-slop-scriptwriting/tree/4c3b6a4bb9b1dbf3ca5c146b7d22db2295abfd80)
project. As the original research notes explain, that project's files,
examples, and linter implementation are not included in this package.
